Crack filling

Asphalt crack filling before a sealcoat, or on its own

Open cracks let water into the pavement. Filling them is a common step before driveway or parking lot sealcoating in this area.

Why cracks get attention first

Asphalt in North Central West Virginia goes through freeze and thaw, sun, and regular traffic. Small cracks can widen, and water that sits in them can weaken the surface underneath. Crack filling, sometimes called crack sealing, is meant to close those gaps on pavement that is still otherwise sound.

Often paired with sealcoating

Many property owners request crack filling and a sealcoat together, then parking lot striping after the coat has set. You can ask for only the cracks if the rest of the surface is already in good shape. If the asphalt has broken into a wide alligator pattern or has potholes, filling alone may not be enough. The form is a good place to describe what you see, and the crew can be honest about whether this is the right work.
