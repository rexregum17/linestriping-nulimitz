How it works

From the first message to a restriped lot or a fresh sealcoat

Here is what to expect when you request line striping or asphalt sealing in North Central West Virginia. There are no prices on this page. The form is the starting point for a quote.

Start with a clear request

Use the quote form to say which town you are in and whether you need parking lot striping, sealcoating, crack filling, or a combination. A short note about the size of the lot or driveway, and whether the lines have faded or the asphalt looks dry and gray, is enough to begin. Photos help a crew see the surface before they visit.

A look at the surface, then a price

A local crew follows up on the request. They may walk the lot or review photos, then they give you a price and a plan before any paint or sealer is applied. What they see matters: a restripe of the current layout is a different job from a new stall pattern, and a driveway that only needs a coat is a different job from a lot that also has open cracks.

Quotes usually depend on size, layout versus restriping, accessible-space markings, oil-spot prep, crack filling, and the weather window. This site does not list rates. Ask any questions about timing or access when the crew replies.

Prep, then the work itself

On the scheduled day, the surface is cleaned so the material can bond. Cracks that should be filled are usually handled before a sealcoat. For striping, the crew either follows the existing lines or lays out a new pattern you have already approved, including arrows, fire lanes, and ADA spaces when those are part of the job.

Time to set before traffic returns

Fresh paint and a fresh sealcoat both need time to set. The wait depends on the product and the weather. The crew should tell you when cars, carts, or trucks can return. If a business has to stay open, mention that on the form so the plan can account for it.

What people usually want to know about the process.

The surface is usually cleaned first so paint or sealer can bond. Oil spots may need extra prep. Loose debris is blown or swept off. If cracks are open, those are often filled before a sealcoat. For restriping, the crew follows the existing layout unless you want a new one.
