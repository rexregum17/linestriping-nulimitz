Sealcoating

Asphalt sealing for driveways and parking lots

A sealcoat is a protective layer for asphalt that is still in good shape. Request a quote for driveway sealcoating or parking lot sealing in North Central WV.

What sealcoating is for

Sealcoating, also called asphalt sealing, is a coat applied to sound asphalt to help protect it from weather, sun, and everyday use. It can also bring back a darker, more even color. It is not a substitute for paving, and it will not fix a surface that has already failed. If the asphalt is still solid and only looks tired, a sealcoat is often the next maintenance step.

Driveways and parking lots

Homeowners usually ask about driveway sealing or driveway sealcoating. Property managers usually ask about parking lot sealcoating, sometimes in the same request as a restripe. Both are the same kind of work, scaled to the surface. Crack filling is often done first so water is less likely to sit in open gaps under the new coat.

Prep, weather, and time to set

The surface needs to be clean, and oil spots may need extra attention, before sealer goes down. Weather matters. A crew will usually look for a dry window and then tell you how long to keep cars off the surface while it sets. If you also want new parking lines, striping is typically scheduled after the sealcoat has cured. You can request both services on the form.

A good candidate is asphalt that is still sound but looks faded, dry, or lightly worn. Open cracks are often filled first. If the pavement is failing, broken, or needs to be replaced, sealcoating is not the right fix. Describe what you see, and the crew can tell you whether a coat is appropriate.

New asphalt usually needs time to cure before a sealcoat. How long depends on the pavement and the season. If the driveway is new, say so on the form so the crew can advise you on timing instead of coating it too soon.
