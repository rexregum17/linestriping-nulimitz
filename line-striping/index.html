When a parking lot needs new lines

Parking lot striping is the painting of stalls, arrows, fire lanes, and other markings on asphalt or concrete. Over time the paint fades. Drivers start to park at odd angles, accessible spaces get harder to see, and the lot looks neglected. A restripe follows the layout you already have. A new layout is for lots that have changed, or for owners who want a cleaner, more efficient pattern.

What a striping job can include

Most commercial lots need standard stalls and drive-lane arrows. Many also need fire lanes, no-parking zones, and ADA or van-accessible spaces with access aisles. If you have reserved stalls, loading zones, or stencils that have worn off, list those in your message. Parking lot painting is the same family of work, just a phrase some property owners use when they search.
